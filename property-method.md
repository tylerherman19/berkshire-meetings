# Property snapshot method

The Property tab (`#property`) is drawn by `property.js` from two kinds of committed file: `property-insights.json`, the region-wide figures precomputed by `python3 property-insights.py` (no network; reads the files below), and one `property-<TOWN_ID>.json` per town, loaded when that town is in focus for the map, the sale-versus-assessment scatter and the records table. The classification rules below are implemented in both `property-insights.py` and `property.js`; change them together, then rerun `property-insights.py`.

Official source: https://www.mass.gov/info-details/massgis-data-property-tax-parcels
Service: https://arcgisserver.digital.mass.gov/arcgisserver/rest/services/AGOL/MassachusettsPropertyTaxParcels/FeatureServer

Collected September 29, 2026, with Python requests. The collector identifies itself as BerkshireMeetings/1.0 with the public repository URL, waits 1.5 seconds before every request, uses no login, proxy or unofficial mirror, pages by OBJECTID in batches of 2,000, and checks row counts. Run `python3 property-collector.py` from the repository. The collector caches raw source responses under .property-cache/*-raw.json and *-geometry.json for interrupted-run recovery; delete those caches before a fresh pull. Raw caches are not committed. Town IDs come from the official table and match the eleven contacts towns.

Assessment table 4, tax-parcel layer 1. Join on TOWN_ID + LOC_ID, not PROP_ID. Assessment rows are the analysis unit. Boundaries are not the analysis unit, and their totals must not be added: a record can join several shapes; a shape can join several records. Shapes are simplified by the official service (0.00002 degrees, five decimal places) for web display, not surveying. A boundary's fill shows the sum of matched usable records; mixed linked uses stay Other / mixed.

22,336 raw rows; 22,316 analyzed. Monterey has 20 rows across six repeated PROP_ID values with conflicting owner labels. All 20 are excluded instead of choosing an owner or double counting. Values and medians use all remaining records, including exempt and zero values. Missing TOTAL_VAL is treated as zero, with the source record retained. Fiscal years vary by town (FY2025-FY2027). Assessments are not market values or tax bills, and the all-record total is not a taxable tax-rate denominator.

Owner grouping: uppercase and whitespace normalization only. No punctuation removal, spelling merges or entity resolution. Public / institutional labels use an explicit keyword screen (town/state/federal, school, church, conservation, university/college). Classification is a lead, not a verified legal status. Private / unclassified labels can include missed institutions. The site separately ranks the largest ten in each screen; complete groups remain in the summary JSON.

Mailing screening: count records with a usable city AND state, then flag non-MA or cities outside the town/alias set. Local aliases: Great Barrington = Housatonic, GT Barrington, GT. Barrington, Great Barrington MA; Egremont = South/North Egremont, SO/NO Egremont; Sheffield = Ashley Falls; New Marlborough = Southfield, Mill River, Hartsville; Becket = North Becket; Otis = East Otis. Other spellings and postal spillover can inflate the result. Mailing address is not residency, vacancy or second-home evidence. This counts assessment records, not owners.

Use groups (Property tab, first three characters of USE_CODE, so four-digit town codes such as 1010 or 130V group with their three-digit parent): 900+ exempt; 130-132, 390-399 and 440-449 vacant land; remaining 100-series residential; 300-499 commercial / industrial; 600-899 farm, forest & recreation (Chapter 61, 61A, 61B current use), as are mixed-use codes below 100 that include a 6, 7 or 8 digit (for example 016, 017, 071); everything else other / mixed. This is a broad code grouping, not verified present-day use. The `category` field inside the per-town files is the collector's older five-group label and is not used by the page. Last recorded sales screen eight-digit YYYYMMDD dates and positive prices and keep nominal transfers; under $1,000 is labeled nominal. Only each record's latest sale exists, so sales-by-year undercounts earlier years (a property resold later drops out of its earlier year). Median price trends use single-family code 101 with a price of $25,000 or more. LLC and trust buyer shares use residential sales of $1,000 or more and a keyword screen on the current owner label (LLC, INC, CORP, LP, LTD, COMPANY, HOLDINGS, PROPERTIES, REALTY and similar; TRUST, TRUSTEE, TR, TTEE, NOMINEE, ESTATE OF and similar). The sale-versus-assessment scatter uses single-family sales of $25,000 or more dated in or after calendar year FY minus 2 of the same record, the window Massachusetts assessors use for that fiscal year, compared with that record's current TOTAL_VAL. None of this is screened for arm's-length status.

Acreage: planar area of each simplified boundary (equirectangular at the ring's latitude), summed per LOC_ID and split evenly among the usable records that share it. NO_MATCH boundaries count toward a town's mapped total but are not attributed. Roads and water outside parcels are not counted. Suitable for shares, not surveying. Value per acre on the map is the sum of linked records' TOTAL_VAL divided by the LOC_ID's mapped area.

Mailing groups on the Property tab: This town (city matches the town or a listed alias, state MA), Elsewhere in MA, New York, Connecticut, Other states, Not listed. "Homes owned from out of state" is New York + Connecticut + Other states over residential records with a listed city and state.

Owner types: Public if the label matches a public-body screen (TOWN OF, INHABITANTS, COMMONWEALTH, COMM OF MASS, STATE OF, UNITED STATES, USA, REGIONAL SCHOOL, SCHOOL DISTRICT, METROPOLITAN DIST, DEPT OF, HOUSING AUTHORITY and similar); otherwise Nonprofit / conservation if it matches a land-trust, conservancy, Audubon, church, college, school, camp, hospital, association or foundation screen or the record carries an exempt use code; otherwise Private. Private land is then split by mailing group. Region-wide landholder totals merge label variants only for these named bodies: Commonwealth of Massachusetts, United States government, Berkshire Natural Resources Council, Mass Audubon, The Nature Conservancy, The Trustees of Reservations, Appalachian Trail Conservancy, Berkshire County Land Trust. Every other owner is grouped by identical normalized label only.

This is a dated committed snapshot, not a live feed. Refresh manually with the collector after checking the town fiscal years; no automated schedule is claimed. Not affiliated with MassGIS or the towns. Data as observed, not a title search.

Review fixes: reject impossible calendar dates and dates after the observation day. Source NO_MATCH=Y overrides a populated LOC_ID: twenty flagged boundaries (15 Becket, one Otis, three Sandisfield, one Tyringham) are outline-only with no value or owner. Homogeneous multi-record use groups retain their actual use category. Tables expose exact chart values; parcel search and the records table provide keyboard access to every boundary. Cached source responses retain their original observation time rather than becoming fresh on rerun.
